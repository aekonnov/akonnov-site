Tree Plus / Mansalto:
an integrated digital loop

Growth and digital transformation of a winery and agricultural enterprise — from production logic and ERP through to booking and digital sales channels.

RoleGrowth, digitalisation and optimisation
BusinessWinemaking · agriculture · hospitality
TechnologyIndustry 4.0 · TeamSystem · Smoobu

Project context

Connecting production with the customer journey

A winery operates several interconnected loops: agricultural and production operations, financial accounting, sales, guest reception and digital communications. Fragmented tools make management difficult and prevent a coherent picture of the business.

Anton's work focused on growth, digitalisation and optimisation of the winery in Italy — including Industry 4.0 initiatives, TeamSystem ERP, the Smoobu booking system and digital sales channels.
