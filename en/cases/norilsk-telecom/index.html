Management challenge

Joining group economics, liquidity and owner decisions

The finance function of a telecommunications group must simultaneously deliver reliable reporting, planning, investment decisions and corporate governance.

Anton's experience covered finance and accounting leadership, budgeting, liquidity management, capital raising, internal control, reorganisation and preparation of materials for boards of directors.
