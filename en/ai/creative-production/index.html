AI accelerates production.
Results pass verification.

In digital production neural networks are not a single-generation button but a set of specialised tools: for research, structure, copy, code, images, video preparation and quality control.

View workflowVerified work

Approach

One project, several model types and checks

A text model helps assemble structure and drafts, a coding agent implements and tests the site, an image generator creates individual visual assets, and the video system prepares files, covers and metadata. Raw facts, material rights and final publish are each controlled separately.

OPS

Publishing system

Ready materials pass to the agent and MCP connectors without exposing tokens in the cloud environment.

Separation of responsibility

Content creation and publishing are different systems

Production owns content, visuals, code and the result file.

Quality gate checks facts, rights, technical quality and behaviour on devices.

Content operations adapts finished material for channels and returns publish links.

Human approves new sensitive content and decisions outside agreed scenarios.

Case boundaries

Real results are shown, not model authorship

AI helps research faster, assemble options and do technical work, but it does not verify facts on its own. Real photographs and approved materials are not replaced by decorative generation when they serve as proof of the project.
