Background

Experience built on own products

The portfolio includes eight self-released board games plus the commissioned Mamoniya project. Experience covers research and content adaptation, mechanics, design, print layouts, production and sales.

Budget: end-to-end board game development starts at ₽1,000,000. Final cost and timeline are set after the brief and depend on mechanics, content volume, number of iterations, components and print run.
