Stage 03 · design

Every component
explains how to play

Board game design simultaneously gives the product character, directs attention and helps the player read the state of play quickly. It evolves alongside mechanics and content.

Discuss designView real work

Stage objective

Not decoration — the interface of a physical product

The visual concept is aligned with the audience, topic and positioning.

The board, cards and tokens receive a unified information hierarchy.

Typography, colour and pictograms help distinguish actions and states.

Components are tested in play trials before final files are prepared.

Workflow

From visual language to a full component set

01

Look

Product character, audience, references and differentiation from competing games.

02

System

Colour, typography, grid, illustrative style and pictograms.

03

Components

Board, cards, tokens, box, rules and supporting materials.

04

Play test

Readability, speed of information retrieval and interaction errors.

05

Freeze

The approved system is handed over to the print-layout stage.

Stage output

A cohesive game visual system

The deliverable is an approved design for all component types, a verified information hierarchy and application rules sufficient for preparing production files.
