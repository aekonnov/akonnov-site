Risks in sight.

When this matters

Leadership learns about problems too late

Risks are discussed ad hoc and are not connected to decisions and the budget.

Controls exist on paper but are not embedded in day-to-day processes.

Accountability is diffuse across departments and management levels.

Reporting is overloaded and key deviations go unnoticed.

The business changes faster than regulations, authorities and the control environment.

A managed system
