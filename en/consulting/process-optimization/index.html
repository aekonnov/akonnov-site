Typical symptoms

The customer waits, departments argue, data diverges

Delivery timelines are unpredictable and delays are discovered only after the fact.

Sales, production, warehousing, logistics and finance operate in separate silos.

Order status is collected manually from emails, spreadsheets and chat messages.

Accountability passes with the task but is never recorded.

ERP or CRM are deployed, yet they do not reflect the real end-to-end process.
