Where projects lose results

The system is in place, yet manageability has not improved

Historical processes are automated without reconsidering their logic.

Business and IT interpret the goal, requirements and readiness criteria differently.

The project depends on the contractor, but there is no internal owner of the result.

Data diverges across systems, spreadsheets and departments.

The expected benefit is expressed in general terms and not verified after go-live.

Transformation logic

Do not start
with technology selection

01
Business outcome

What should become faster, more transparent, cheaper or more reliable.

02
Process and data

How work is done today and what information is needed to make decisions.

03
Target model

Roles, rules, control, integrations and acceptance criteria.

04
Technology and implementation

Solution selection, pilot, contractors, training and transition.
